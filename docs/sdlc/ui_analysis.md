## Step-by-step flows

1. **Sign-in flow — REQ-001–REQ-008, REQ-019 [A, S, K].** Open **Sign in** (only its static composition is **Observed [A]**). **Proposed (inferred, not observed):** type synthetic email and password into accessible, actually masked fields; submit. Empty/invalid inputs receive non-secret-disclosing field or form feedback. Valid credentials reach **Authenticated result** backed by an active session. Following “Forgot password?” instead opens **Reset request**; the destination is unverified in the image.
2. **Request flow — REQ-004, REQ-005, REQ-009–REQ-012, REQ-019 [A, S, K].** **Proposed (inferred, not observed):** from **Sign in**, follow the link to **Reset request**, type an account email and submit. **Generic request acknowledgement** presents the same public message and outcome for known and unknown accounts and for a known account after its fifth accepted request in the rolling hour; no link, existence signal or quota signal appears. For an eligible known synthetic account, a usable link is issued into the private local in-memory sink only; the sixth request within the rolling hour issues no additional link. No invented quota for unknown accounts is assumed.
3. **Completion flow — REQ-010, REQ-013–REQ-017, REQ-019 [S, K].** **Proposed (inferred, not observed):** an authorized local demonstrator obtains a known synthetic account's link from the in-memory sink, **not** from the public acknowledgement; opening an unused link before 30 minutes from issuance shows **New password**. Submit a new masked password. An invalid, consumed or expired link (at or after 30 minutes from issue) leads to **Invalid-link state** without updating the password. On a valid submission, consume the link once, update the password, invalidate **every** old account session, record an owner-addressed in-memory notification after that successful reset, and show **Reset success** with a return to **Sign in**. New credentials work; old sessions remain unauthenticated. No real email or public token display is implied.

## Proposed states and accessibility (all inferred, not observed)

These recommendations [A, S, K] do not certify that the screenshot meets contrast, keyboard or accessibility requirements. Every screen should have a descriptive heading and logical reading/focus order, visible keyboard focus, sufficient text/control/focus contrast, keyboard-operable links/buttons, and names for interactive elements that a screen reader can announce. Associate labels with fields (a placeholder alone is not a label); attach error messages to the affected fields, identify them in text as well as color, and announce form-level error/success/progress via appropriate status or focus placement without revealing secrets. Route changes should move focus to the new heading or a meaningful error summary.

## Assumptions carried forward

- **A1 [S]:** Locally defined synthetic credentials suffice for a demonstrable sign-in/reset/session flow; no extra password policy, production identity provider, persistent store or exact post-sign-in UX is specified.
- **A2 [S, A]:** Email is the reset-request identifier, consistent with the pictured email field; reset pages are simply server-rendered and their exact design and error copy are unspecified.
- **A3 [S, K]:** Links for known synthetic accounts and notifications after successful resets are inspected only via the in-memory sink for local demonstration/tests; no real email and no public reset-response links.
- **A4 [K]:** Interpret the five-per-account-per-hour known-account limit as rolling; unknown identifiers receive the same public response and no invented numeric quota.
- **A5 [K]:** An unused link can be used before 30 minutes measured from issuance and is invalid at or after that point; exact boundary interpretation is an assumption, not quoted policy.
